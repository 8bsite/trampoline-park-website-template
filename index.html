One roof, six ways off the ground

Every zone has its own coach, its own rules and its own kind of noise. Your wristband moves you between them for as long as your session lasts.

  1. Free Jump Arena

    Forty linked beds and angled walls you can run up. The main hall, the loudest room, the one everybody starts in.

    All ages

  2. Foam Pit

    Nine thousand foam blocks, deep enough to swallow a grown-up. Practise the flip here first; nobody is watching, honestly.

    Ages 6+

  3. Target Wall

    A wall of light-up targets. Hit them mid-bounce before they go dark; the scoreboard remembers your name.

    Ages 7+

  4. Ninja Wall

    Twelve obstacles, one stopwatch and a padded floor for when your grip gives up. The fastest times go on the league table.

    Ages 8+

  5. Slam Lanes

    Three trampoline runways that end at hoops of three heights. Everyone dunks here, even the shortest person in your group.

    Ages 6+

  6. Little Hops

    A quieter corner of small beds, soft walls and slow music, kept for the under-fives and the grown-ups holding their hands.

    Under 5s

House rules, short version

  • Grip socks on

    Ours are red, cost £2.50 and are yours to keep. Bare feet and normal socks slip.

  • One per bed

    One jumper on each trampoline at a time. The coaches will remind you, loudly and kindly.

  • Flips with a coach

    Somersaults only in the foam pit or with a coach watching. Everywhere else, feet first.

  • Pockets empty

    Phones, keys and coins go in the free lockers. Trampoline springs eat them.

  • Waiver first

    Every jumper needs a signed waiver. Do it online before you come and skip the queue.

Do I need to book?

Booking online is the only way to be sure of a slot, especially at weekends and in school holidays. Walk-ins are welcome when there is space.

How old do you have to be?

Little Hops is for under-fives with a grown-up. Open Jump suits ages five and up, Glow Night is 13 and up, and grown-ups can jump at any session.

Do grown-ups have to jump?

No. Spectators are free and the Landing café looks right over the main arena. Children under eight need a grown-up in the building.

What should we wear?

Comfortable clothes without zips, buckles or belts, plus our grip socks. Take off jewellery and empty your pockets into a free locker.

Is there a waiver?

Yes. Every jumper needs one, signed by a parent or carer if they are under 18. It takes two minutes online and lasts a year.

Can we bring our own food?

Only birthday cakes. The Landing serves hot food, snacks and drinks all day, and can cater for most allergies if you ask at the counter.

Is Hopla accessible?

There is step-free access to the café, the toilets and two of the courts, a quiet hour every Sunday at nine and free entry for carers.

Where do we park?

Forty free spaces in Furnace Yard, and the Shalesmoor tram stop is a two-minute walk away.
